## Supervision

I am happy to hear from students interested in projects on combined-probe cosmology, large-scale structure theory, or the computational side of cosmological inference — emulators, differentiable pipelines and simulation-based methods. The best way to reach me is by email.

If you are a secondary school student rather than an undergraduate, you may find my page of [resources and advice]({{ '/advice/' | relative_url }}) more useful.
