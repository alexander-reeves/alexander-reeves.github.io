## Multiprobe frameworks

I build end-to-end, differentiable pipelines that combine multiple cosmological probes (weak lensing, galaxy clustering, BAO, CMB primary anisotropies, and CMB lensing) in a single, self-consistent inference. This includes simulation-based covariances, fast emulators, and cross-correlation measurements to maximise information while controlling systematics across datasets.

## EFTofLSS galaxy clustering

I work on full-shape galaxy clustering using the Effective Field Theory of Large-Scale Structure (EFTofLSS), with an emphasis on modern, differentiable tooling (e.g., PyBird-JAX) and neural network emulation. The goal is to extract robust information from quasi-linear scales and to integrate these measurements seamlessly into multiprobe analyses.

## Beyond ΛCDM constraints

I develop model-independent and model-flexible approaches to test dark energy and gravity, constrain the summed neutrino mass, and probe early-Universe physics. This includes Gaussian Process reconstructions of expansion and growth histories, and mapping reconstructed power spectra to primordial initial conditions to test inflationary predictions.
